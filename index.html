How it plays

Three taps from calm to catch

A relaxed loop you can pick up in a minute and play in the time it takes the kettle to boil.

Cast

Press on your fisherman, pull back like a slingshot, and let go. Aim for the ripples: bigger rings mean bigger fish.

Reel

Tap when the arrow sits in the Green. Yellow is fine, Red hurts. Every fish fights in its own way, from calm to darting to downright sneaky.

Collect

Keep your best catches, sell the rest to Old Marlow, and craft permanent charms from the fish you've sold.

What's in the boat

A whole world to fish through

Five islands, a bar full of jobs, and a stubborn old mentor who misses the water.

100

Fish to find

Fill your journal one silhouette at a time. Every species has its own size record, and some of them are legendary.

5

Islands to sail

From Pebble Pond to the deep reef. Finish Marlow's jobs, earn medals, and unlock the next boat. Watch out for the giants out at sea.

✦

Charms you craft

Sell the right fish and Marlow makes you a permanent charm: calmer fish, wider Green zones, even a second wind when the line goes slack.

☀

Time and weather

Pick the hour and watch the sky. Mornings bite more often; dusk and night hide the rare ones.

⌂

A home of your own

Hang your record catches on the wall, furnish the room, and change how your fisherman looks in the wardrobe.

♥

A pup who's yours

On your first trip you hook something that isn't a fish. He becomes your partner and barks when something good is biting.

Meet your partner

You went fishing. You caught a dog.

On your very first cast, something heavy takes the hook, and it's wearing a red bandana. Marlow laughs, the pup wags, and from then on he trots along beside you on the island and sits by the pier while you fish.

He's small, he's loyal, and he knows when a good one is biting.
