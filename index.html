1 · A hundred species, one journal

Collect 100 kinds of fish

From sleepy pond minnows to golden koi, every island hides its own fish. Some only bite at night, some only in a storm, and every one has a size record waiting to be beaten.

Fill the journal page by page, hang your proudest catches on the wall, and sell the right fish to old Marlow to have him craft you lucky charms.

2 · Giants of the deep

Fight legendary creatures

Sail somewhere new and something huge rises to meet you. Four legendary creatures guard the way to the far islands, and each one takes real nerve to land.

Win the reel, again and again, until the giant gives up. Then see how big it really was.

3 · You went fishing. You caught a dog.

Go fishing with your best friend

Your very first cast hooks something heavy, and it's wearing a red bandana with an old fisherman's charm tied to it. Nobody on the island knows whose it is.

So he comes along: sitting by the pier while you wait for a bite, and leaping in to bite the giants when you need a paw. And somewhere out past the last giant, someone has been missing him.

4 · Dress the part

Your fashion, your way!

Pick your skin, eyes, hair and its colour, an outfit, bottoms, shoes and a hat, glasses or earrings, and spin your fisher around to see the whole look.

Help the islanders and they'll thank you with special outfits and masks to add to your wardrobe.

5 · A home of your own

Decorate your house

Furnish your cottage piece by piece: beds, sofas, rugs, lamps and plants from the furniture shop, plus presents from the islanders for helping out.

Tap to pick a piece up, drag it where you want it, and hang your biggest catches on the wall.

The water's calm. Your pup's waiting.
