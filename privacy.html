Fisherman's Charm

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Fisherman's Charm is a single-player fishing game made by Shenna Widianta. It is built to work without collecting anything about you. This page says exactly what that means.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics or tracking inside the game.

Your saved game

Your progress is saved on your own device. If iCloud is turned on for the game, your saves are also kept in your own iCloud account so they follow you to your other devices. That data is stored and managed by Apple under your Apple ID. We cannot see it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game can show your achievements and post your scores to the leaderboards. This is handled entirely by Apple's Game Center service, under Apple's privacy policy. We only send Game Center the achievement progress and scores you earn in the game.

In-app purchase

The game offers one optional purchase that unlocks the full game. The purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, only whether the purchase was made.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

The website for the game uses GoatCounter to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. It records anonymous page views, the referring site and a rough device type. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
